SARWAR.CYOU — Logo Color & Animation Variations

Explore 6 refined color treatments and animation choreographies for the master wordmark. Each variation permanently eliminates the jarring "black and yellow" flashing cycle by maintaining steady base luminance and replacing dark murky tones with vivid specular light.

Root Cause Analysis: Why The Logo Was Alternating Black & Yellow
The previous gradient had dark boundary stops set to #050805 (near-black) and #557700 (olive green) layered as an opaque stroke directly over the letters. As the gradient animated horizontally, each letterform was repeatedly masked in solid black before flashing back to neon lime, creating an unintended strobe-like alternation.

The Solution: In every variation below, the base letters are anchored in steady, vibrant illumination. The moving wave is a pure specular glint, a fluid multi-color spectrum, or an organic breathing pulse — with zero black dips.
